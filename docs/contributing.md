# Contributing to TaurusTLS

You can contribute to TaurusTLS by:

- [Making a donation](https://opencollective.com/TaurusTLS-Developers) to help cover the costs of TaurusTLS.

- Giving the [TaurusTLS repository](https://github.com/TaurusTLS-Developers/TaurusTLS) a star.

- Contribute Source-code to TaurusTLS using [Pull-Requests](https://github.com/TaurusTLS-Developers/TaurusTLS/pulls).

- Making [bug reports for issues](https://github.com/TaurusTLS-Developers/TaurusTLS/issues) that you find.

- Linking to the [TaurusTLS website](https://taurustls.org/) from your website.

- Having your TaurusTLS-based application listed on our [Links Page](https://taurustls.org/links.xhtml) by letting us know through the [Show And Tell Forum discussion forum](https://github.com/orgs/TaurusTLS-Developers/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).
